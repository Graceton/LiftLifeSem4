TRICEP DIPS EXERCISE

Tricep dips are an effective exercise for building strength in the triceps.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Position your hands on a bench or chair behind you, with your fingers facing forward.
  2. Extend your legs out in front of you, keeping your heels on the ground.
  3. Lower your body by bending your elbows until they are at a 90-degree angle.
  4. Push back up to the starting position.
