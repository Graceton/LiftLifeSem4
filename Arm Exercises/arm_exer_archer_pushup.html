ARCHER PUSH-UPS EXERCISE

Archer push-ups are an advanced variation that targets the chest, shoulders, and triceps.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Start in a push-up position with your hands wider than shoulder-width apart.
  2. Shift your weight to one side and lower your body towards that hand.
  3. Push back up and repeat on the other side.
