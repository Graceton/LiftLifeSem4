CHILD'S POSE

Child's pose is a restful position that stretches the back and hips.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Kneel on the floor with your big toes touching and knees apart.
  2. Sit back on your heels and extend your arms forward on the ground.
  3. Relax your forehead on the mat and breathe deeply.
