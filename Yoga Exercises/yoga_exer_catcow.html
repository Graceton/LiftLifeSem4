CAT-COW POSE

Cat-Cow pose is a gentle flow between two poses that warms the body and brings flexibility to the spine.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Start on your hands and knees in a tabletop position.
  2. Inhale as you arch your back, dropping your belly towards the mat (Cow Pose).
  3. Exhale as you round your spine, tucking your chin to your chest (Cat Pose).
