KING PIGEON POSE

King pigeon pose is an advanced pose that opens the hips and stretches the thighs.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Start in a tabletop position.
  2. Bring your right knee forward and place it behind your right wrist.
  3. Extend your left leg back, keeping your hips square.
  4. Lower your torso over your right leg and hold the position.
