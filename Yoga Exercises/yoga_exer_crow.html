CROW POSE

Crow pose is an arm balance that builds strength and focus.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Start in a squat position with your feet close together.
  2. Place your hands on the ground in front of you, shoulder-width apart.
  3. Shift your weight forward and lift your feet off the ground, balancing on your hands.
