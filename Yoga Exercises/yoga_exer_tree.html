TREE POSE

Tree pose is a balancing pose that strengthens the legs and improves focus.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Stand tall with your feet together.
  2. Shift your weight onto one leg and place the other foot on your inner thigh or calf.
  3. Bring your hands to your heart center or raise them overhead.
