HEADSTAND

Headstand is an advanced pose that builds strength and improves circulation.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Start on your knees and interlace your fingers behind your head.
  2. Place the crown of your head on the mat and lift your hips up.
  3. Slowly walk your feet in towards your body and lift your legs up towards the ceiling.
