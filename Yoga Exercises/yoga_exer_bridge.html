BRIDGE POSE

Bridge pose is a great exercise for strengthening the back and opening the chest.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat on the floor.
  2. Press your feet into the ground and lift your hips towards the ceiling.
  3. Hold the position for a few breaths, then lower back down.
