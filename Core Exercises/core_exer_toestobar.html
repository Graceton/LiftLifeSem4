TOES TO BAR EXERCISE

Toes to bar is an advanced exercise that targets the entire core and hip flexors.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Hang from a pull-up bar with your legs straight down.
  2. Raise your legs and touch your toes to the bar.
  3. Lower your legs back down with control.
