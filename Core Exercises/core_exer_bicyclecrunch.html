ADVANCED CRUNCH EXERCISE

Bicycle crunches are an effective exercise for targeting the abdominal muscles.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Lie on your back with your hands behind your head and legs raised.
  2. Bring your right elbow to your left knee while extending your right leg.
  3. Switch sides and repeat.
