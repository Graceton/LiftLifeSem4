PLANCK HOLDS EXERCISE

Planche holds are an advanced exercise that requires significant core strength and stability.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Start in a push-up position with your feet together.
  2. Lower your body down while keeping your elbows close to your sides.
  3. Hold the position for as long as possible.
