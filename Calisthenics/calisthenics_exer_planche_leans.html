PLANche LEANS

Planche leans are an advanced exercise that builds strength in the shoulders and core.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Start in a push-up position with your hands slightly wider than shoulder-width.
  2. Lean your body forward while keeping your elbows straight.
  3. Hold the position for a few seconds, then return to the starting position.
