PISTOL SQUATS

Pistol squats are an advanced exercise that builds strength and balance in the legs.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Stand on one leg with the other leg extended in front of you.
  2. Lower your body into a squat while keeping the extended leg off the ground.
  3. Push back up to the starting position.
