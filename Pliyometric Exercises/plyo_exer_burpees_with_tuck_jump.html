BURPEES WITH TUCK JUMP

Burpees with tuck jump increase the intensity of the traditional burpee.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Start in a standing position.
  2. Drop into a squat position with your hands on the ground.
  3. Kick your feet back into a push-up position.
  4. Return to the squat position and jump up explosively, tucking your knees to your chest.
