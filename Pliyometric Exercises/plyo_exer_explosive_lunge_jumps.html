EXPLOSIVE LUNGE JUMPS

Explosive lunge jumps are a dynamic exercise that develops asymmetrical power.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Start in a lunge position with one foot forward and the other back.
  2. Explode upwards, switching your legs in mid-air.
  3. Land softly in a lunge position with the opposite leg forward.
