SINGLE-LEG BOX JUMPS

Single-leg box jumps are an advanced exercise that adds unilateral strength and control.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Stand in front of a sturdy box or platform.
  2. Shift your weight onto one leg and prepare to jump.
  3. Jump onto the box, landing softly on the same leg.
