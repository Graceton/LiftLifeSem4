PLYOMETRIC PUSH-UPS

Plyometric push-ups are an explosive upper body exercise that builds strength and power.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Start in a push-up position.
  2. Lower your body towards the ground.
  3. Explode upwards, pushing off the ground and clapping your hands.
  4. Land softly and go into the next push-up.
