LUNGES EXERCISE

Lunges are a great exercise for building leg strength and improving balance.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart.
  2. Step forward with one leg, lowering your hips until both knees are bent at about a 90-degree angle.
  3. Push back to the starting position and repeat with the other leg.
