STEP-UPS EXERCISE

Step-ups are a great exercise for building leg strength and improving balance.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Stand in front of a bench or step.
  2. Step up with one foot, pressing through your heel.
  3. Bring the other foot up to meet the first on the bench.
  4. Step back down and repeat with the other leg.
