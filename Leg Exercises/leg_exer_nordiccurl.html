NORDIC CURLS EXERCISE

Nordic curls are an advanced exercise that targets the hamstrings and builds strength.

STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION:

  1. Kneel on a soft surface with your feet secured under a stable object.
  2. Lower your body forward as slowly as possible.
  3. Use your hamstrings to pull yourself back up to the starting position.
